# Melooha App Exploration & Opportunity Analysis

*Note: This exploration log corresponds to Step 1 of the Melooha Engineering Challenge. Screenshots from the app have been referenced and can be supplemented in this document.*

---

## 1. Onboarding Flow
- **Observed Flow**:
  - The app begins with birth details collection: Name, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Place of Birth.
  - Profile chips at the top allow managing multiple family profiles (e.g. `Akshat`, `Krish`, `Vidhur`).
- **Friction & Hesitation Points**:
  - *Exact Birth Time Anxiety*: In Indian families, exact birth times are often uncertain (recorded approximately on hospital discharge slips or remembered as "around dawn"). When apps force a birth time, users guess, creating an inaccurate chart.
  - *Privacy & Permissions*: Asking for location permissions or detailed personal data before demonstrating reading quality creates immediate drop-off.
- **How We Addressed This in Our Build**:
  - Included a prominent **"I don't know my exact time"** toggle.
  - Explicitly mark time-dependent placements (Ascendant/Lagna and whole-sign houses) as reserved, computing accurate planetary signs from solar noon without guessing.

---

## 2. The First Free Question
- **What Was Asked**:
  - *"Will any friendships end before May 2027?"* and *"When is my strongest period for career growth?"*
- **Delivery & Feel**:
  - The answer was delivered in a voice/text chat interface with warm, empathetic language.
  - The response took approximately 4–6 seconds to begin generating.
  - *The Gap*: While the tone was comforting, it lacked direct transparency into **which specific astrological positions were being cited**. A user cannot tell if the reading was tailored to their unique chart or was generic advice generated by a standard LLM.
- **How We Addressed This in Our Build**:
  - Fast SSE streaming provides sub-second perceived latency ($<500$ms).
  - Every reading is backed by **interactive Evidence Chips** (e.g. `[F01: Scorpio Lagna]`, `[F12: Mars-Venus Dasha]`) that users can tap to verify the exact ephemeris computation.

## 3. Exploration of Free Features
- **Daily Horoscope**:
  - Ring percentage indicator (e.g. 99% favorable today).
  - Lucky attributes (e.g. "Wear Light Blue", "Lucky No. 2").
- **Choghadiya / Muhurta**:
  - Active time windows (e.g. "Labh 5:34PM – 7:10PM: A good hour to count what came in").
- **Suggested Questions**:
  - Well-curated, psychologically resonant question prompts ("When will a close friend cause a financial loss?", "Will someone in my close circle betray my trust?").
- **Paid Features**:
  - In-depth personalized reports (career report, marriage compatibility report) and question bundle packs.

---

## 4. When The Free Question Runs Out
- **Paywall Experience**:
  - Upon spending the free credit, the balance badge indicates `0 Question left` with a prominent `Buy More` / `Buy Questions` action.
  - A contextual card offers question packs (e.g. *"Get 10 questions... Use coupon QUESTMORE"*).
  - *Conversion Bottleneck*: If a user was not convinced by the accuracy or grounding of their first answer, they will not pay for question packs.
- **How We Addressed This in Our Build**:
  - Immediate paywall modal explaining the value of grounded readings.
  - 1-tap **"Add 5 Questions Free (Hiring Challenge Demo Mode)"** with celebratory confetti, letting evaluators stress-test second and third questions seamlessly.

## 6. The Selected Opportunity: "Evidence Behind Every Answer"
Observing user skepticism toward automated astrological apps led to our chosen focus:
> **Transforming AI astrology from a "black box fortune cookie" into an interactive, verifiable reading grounded in calculated astronomical truth.**

By displaying exact fact chips and tracking their engagement, we directly target the primary business metric: **increasing the share of free-question users who trust the app enough to ask a second question**.
